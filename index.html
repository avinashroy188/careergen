How does the AI Literature Camp work?

Our 8-week program combines simple explanations with hands-on activities. Students attend three classes per week two concept sessions and one practical session and build their skills step by step.

Does my child need prior AI or coding experience?

One-on-one gives you a pace and plan built entirely around you, while group sessionNot at all! The course starts with AI basics and gradually introduces prompting, creative tools, design, and beginner-friendly coding.s add peer energy at a lower cost. Your mentor will recommend the best fit after the free first session.

Is the course suitable for my child's age?

The program is designed for students in Classes 7–10. Activities are structured to be engaging, age-appropriate, and easy for beginners to follow.

What will my child create during the course?

Students experiment with AI-generated content, explore creative design, learn basic HTML and CSS, and build their own portfolio website to showcase their projects.

How do you track student progress?

Progress is demonstrated through practical exercises, mini-projects, feedback, and the final portfolio presentation
